ACSR 380/50 mm² passes all 17 type tests at EHVRC

The Extra High Voltage Research Center of the Egyptian Electricity Holding Company issued report 568/2026 on our ACSR 380/50 mm² conductor. The sample passed every test in the programme: surface condition, inertness, wrapping of aluminium and zinc-coated wires, design and construction, direction and ratio of lay, resistivity, zinc-coating mass, mass per unit length, zinc dip and adhesion, wire tensile strength, steel-wire elongation, stress at 1 % extension, the conductor tensile test at 95 % of RTS, and joints in aluminium wires.
