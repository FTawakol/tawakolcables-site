Altawakol for Cables

Altawakol for Cables is the group's cable-manufacturing company, producing bare overhead conductors and low-voltage cables for Egypt's distribution utilities, contractors and EPC partners, and for export.

Backed by the engineering depth of Al Tawakol Group, from power panels and substations to transmission towers and galvanization, we understand the network our conductors are built for.

What we offer

  • AAC, AAAC and ACSR from 16 mm² to 1000 mm²
  • Metric and BS code-name sizes in one range
  • Manufacture to IEC, BS EN, ES or customer specification
  • Type, sample and routine tests with full reports
  • Wooden or steel drums, lengths to suit your stringing plan
  • Support for conductor selection and sag-tension

Backed by Al Tawakol Group

Its companies span electrical solutions, steel fabrication and galvanization, transmission towers, renewable energy and distribution. Altawakol for Cables is the group's cable-manufacturing company. How we make it

Every conductor follows a controlled route from rod to drum, with in-process checks at each stage and final testing before dispatch.

Aluminium rod

Electrical-grade aluminium (EC/1350) or Al-Mg-Si alloy rod is received, sampled and checked for chemistry and conductivity.

Wire drawing

Rod is drawn through multi-die machines to the exact wire diameter, with continuous diameter and surface control.

Heat treatment

Alloy wires for AAAC are precipitation-hardened to reach the strength and conductivity required by EN 50183 / IEC 60104.

Steel core

Galvanized steel wires for ACSR are inspected for tensile strength, elongation and zinc-coating mass before stranding.

Stranding

Wires are laid in concentric layers on rigid stranders with controlled lay ratios, alternating direction and a right-hand outer layer.

Testing and drumming

Finished conductor is measured, tested, wound under controlled tension onto drums, sealed, lagged and labelled.
