AAAC All Aluminium Alloy Conductor

AAAC conductors are stranded from heat-treated aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy wires (AL3 / 6201-T81). They combine high mechanical strength with good conductivity, giving lower sag and longer spans than AAC without the corrosion risk of a steel core.

Applications

  • MV distribution and HV transmission lines
  • Long spans and reduced tower count
  • Coastal, desert and industrial environments
  • Re-conductoring of existing ACSR routes

Construction

  • Al-Mg-Si alloy wires AL3 to EN 50183 / IEC 60104
  • 7, 19, 37, 61 or 91 wires
  • Alternate-direction layers, right-hand outer layer
  • Higher strength-to-weight ratio than ACSR
