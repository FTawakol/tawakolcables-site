ACSR Aluminium Conductor Steel Reinforced

ACSR conductors consist of one or more layers of hard-drawn aluminium wires (AL1) stranded over a core of galvanized high-strength steel wires (ST1A). The steel core carries the mechanical load while the aluminium carries the current: the proven choice for long spans and heavy loading.

Applications

  • HV and EHV transmission lines
  • MV distribution lines with long spans
  • River and valley crossings
  • Areas with high wind or ice loading

Construction

  • Aluminium wires AL1 to IEC 60889
  • Galvanized steel core ST1A, class A zinc coating
  • Steel core of 1 or 7 wires
  • Core or full-body grease to EN 50326 on request
