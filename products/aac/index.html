AAC All Aluminium Conductor

AAC conductors are made of hard-drawn electrical-grade aluminium wires (AL1 / 1350, min. 99.5 % Al) concentrically stranded in one or more layers. They offer the highest conductivity-to-weight ratio of any bare conductor and inherently good corrosion resistance.

Applications

  • Urban and rural MV/LV distribution lines
  • Short spans where high current is needed
  • Substation busbars and jumpers
  • Coastal areas with moderate corrosion

Construction

  • Aluminium wires AL1 to IEC 60889
  • 7, 19, 37, 61 or 91 wires
  • Alternate-direction layers, right-hand outer layer
  • Optional grease protection to EN 50326
