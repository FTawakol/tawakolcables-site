Inside the 380/50

The conductor on the 220 and 500 kV lines, layer by layer, exactly as the laboratory measured it.

  1. 7 × 3.00 mm galvanized steel core, ST1A, class A zinc. Carries the mechanical load.
  2. 12 + 18 + 24 × 3.00 mm hard-drawn AL1 aluminium in three layers. Carries the current: 820 A at 80 °C.
  3. Alternating lay, right-hand outer lay ratio 12.66 measured against a 10 to 14 requirement.
  4. 269 g/m² zinc coating on the steel, against a 245 g/m² minimum: 10 % more protection than the standard asks.
