Ordering

To receive an accurate quotation, include the conductor type, size or code name, standard, total length and length per drum, drum type, grease and inspection requirements.

Example: ACSR 240/40 to BS EN 50182 · 60 km in 2,000 m lengths · non-returnable wooden drums · core greased.
