Quality & testing

Quality is designed into each stage of production. Raw materials, wires and finished conductors are tested in our laboratory against the applicable standard, and every drum ships with its test certificate.

Applicable standards

IEC 61089Round wire concentric lay overhead electrical stranded conductors
BS EN 50182Conductors for overhead lines: round wire concentric lay stranded conductors
IEC 60889Hard-drawn aluminium wire for overhead line conductors (AL1)
EN 50183 / IEC 60104Aluminium-magnesium-silicon alloy wire (AL2 / AL3)
EN 50189 / IEC 60888Zinc-coated steel wires for stranded conductors (ST1A)
ESEgyptian Standards for bare overhead conductors
ASTMB230/B231, B398/B399, B232, on request

Wire tests

  • Diameter and surface condition
  • Tensile strength and elongation
  • Resistivity / conductivity
  • Wrapping and torsion (ductility)
  • Zinc-coating mass and adherence (steel)

Conductor tests

  • Lay ratio and lay direction
  • Mass per unit length and overall diameter
  • DC resistance at 20 °C
  • Rated tensile strength (type test)
  • Stress–strain characteristics (type test)

Type test: ACSR 380/50 mm²

A sample of our ACSR 380/50 conductor was type-tested by the Extra High Voltage Research Center (EHVRC), Laboratories, Research and Testing Sector, Egyptian Electricity Holding Company. All 17 tests met the requirements of IEC 61089, IEC 62641, IEC 63248, BS EN 50182, BS EN 50189 and EDMS 04-100-1.
